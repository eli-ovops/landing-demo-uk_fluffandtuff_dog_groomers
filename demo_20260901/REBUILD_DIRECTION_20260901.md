# Rebuild direction - customer-content correction

**Status:** `FAIL - REDESIGN DIRECTION` for the prior generic-concept version. This document records the authorised local rebuild direction.

## Why the previous draft failed

The previous draft used one 100px Instagram profile image and three generic concept images. It omitted the customer's already public owned website, official Facebook page, actual gallery photos, full service detail, real home-salon context and verified social ecosystem. It therefore had industry recognition but weak customer ownership.

## Selected direction

**Design Read:** Welling home-grooming website concept for dog owners who value a calm, personal appointment, with an honest editorial service-journal language and a real-work photography-first composition.

| Dial | Value | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `DESIGN_VARIANCE` | 7 | An asymmetrical image-led hero, varied service layouts and photo-led proof avoid the generic salon-card template. |
| `MOTION_INTENSITY` | 3 | Static photography and direct reading take priority. Hover/focus feedback only. |
| `VISUAL_DENSITY` | 6 | The owner has useful service, environment, gallery and contact content. The page should feel complete without becoming a full catalogue. |
| `SOUL` | Familiar, hands-on, personable | The business is a home-based, one-to-one groomer. It should feel warm through real work, not pastel decoration. |
| `SPECTACLE` | 3 | Real client photography is the hero engine. No automatic animation is necessary. |

**Default rejected:** pastel paw-print spa, centered copy, three matching service cards, fake rating widgets, and generic AI dog photographs.
