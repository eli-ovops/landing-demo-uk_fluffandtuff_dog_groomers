# P3 Page skeleton

1. **Hero — recognition:** business name, `Dog grooming in Welling`, and the verified `one-to-one, home-based` framing. One original concept hero image shows the category, not a claimed Fluffandtuff result.
2. **Service rail — orientation:** three selected, rechecked booking-catalogue services. Each service receives a concise plain-language description that does not add technique, price, duration, or outcome claims.
3. **Care / proof panel — confidence:** a concise statement that the business's current client reviews and booking availability can be viewed on the external booking service. No copied review text, reviewer identity, or unrefreshed numerical rating.
4. **Visual field — relevance:** distinct original concept imagery plus the registered official Instagram profile surface; the image ledger makes their different roles explicit.
5. **Closing — action:** location-level Welling recognition and an outbound existing booking link. No self-hosted booking or hours.

The final section sequence must be checked against the actual visual implementation; this is a commercial-content skeleton, not customer-visible copy.
