# Pre-code decision - bright home-studio rebuild

## Authority and boundary

This is an authorised local static concept rebuild. It changes no public page, booking account, outreach or source-transfer entitlement. `CUSTOMER AUTHORIZATION: NOT VERIFIED` remains in the internal asset records.

## Customer truth and commercial cut

The owned website and official Facebook page identify a one-to-one, home-based Welling groomer run by Christina. Public claims used here are: calm individual appointments, the garden-studio setting, Puppy groom, Full groom, Bath and de-shed, 10+ years' experience, City & Guilds Level 3 qualification, an accessible electric table and the existing Fresha route. Their URLs and source bounds are in [OWNED_SITE_CORRECTION_20260901.md](../research_20260901/OWNED_SITE_CORRECTION_20260901.md).

The current site presents these facts across Home, About, Services, Gallery and Testimonials. This concept's specific one-page increment is a faster first-visit route: a real dog, the home setting, appointment choices, care evidence, work samples and the existing Fresha route appear in one scroll. It does not claim the business lacks a website or need a booking system.

## Design read

`Design Read: independent Welling dog groomer · sunny, generous, home-studio confidence · register=brand · SOUL=8 · SPECTACLE=3 · DENSITY=6 · hero-engine=static photographic composition.`

The lazy default is pastel paw prints, beige pet-spa serenity or faux luxury salon black. We reject all three. The selected Soul is a bright neighbourhood studio: clear sky-blue surfaces, a single lively blue accent, generous rounded photography and direct, reassuring copy. Natural warmth comes from real dogs and the garden-studio facts, not craft-beige decoration.

## Two directions considered

1. **Selected - sunny home-studio.** A bright blue field, rounded portrait frames, friendly sans display type and an appointment-first scroll path. It reflects one-to-one care without becoming childish.
2. **Rejected - boutique editorial salon.** Dark neutrals, oversized typographic drama and stark gallery presentation would suppress the approachable family-studio personality the user selected.

## Photo-density correction

User visual review found cropped dog heads, oversized type, large blank colour fields and text-only social links. The corrected build reduces visible prose, uses all eight official dog photographs as primary visual content, gives each a crop selected for the dog's face, and replaces flat panels with two distinct conceptual blurred garden-bokeh assets: FTD-13 in the closing visit panel and FTD-16 in the social links panel. FTD-14 and FTD-15 are official platform marks for the outbound Instagram and Facebook controls. Neither concept background is described as the customer's real garden.

## 9-point refinement

The final visual refinement makes the text wordmark read as a local-service lock-up, adds only sourced service identity (`1-to-1`, `Home studio care`) as a compact photo caption, and shortens the hero and studio body copy. It does not introduce a claimed official logo, testimonials, ratings, opening hours, prices, new service facts, or any dynamic feature.
